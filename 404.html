Tikipedia

Wikipedia, as short videos

Tikipedia turns Wikipedia into a vertical short-form feed: swipe for the next article, hear it narrated with your device’s voice, and follow along with word-by-word captions.

Articles are fetched live from Wikimedia. A local recommendation engine learns from what you watch, like, and open — preferences stay on your device. Install it as an app for quick access (an internet connection is required).

Originally by rebane2001. Source on GitHub.

Note to iOS users: if narration or install feels flaky, try Safari desktop mode or another device.
