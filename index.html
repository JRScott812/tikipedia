Xikipedia

Wikipedia, as short videos

Xikipedia turns Simple Wikipedia into a vertical short-form feed: swipe for the next article, hear it narrated with your device’s voice, and follow along with word-by-word captions.

A local recommendation engine learns from what you watch, like, and open — nothing leaves your device. After the first load, it works offline and can be installed as an app.

Originally by rebane2001. Source on GitHub.

Note to iOS users: Apple limits how much memory a site can use, so loading may crash on some iPhones. Try desktop mode in Safari, or use another device if it still fails.

    Xikipedia is a short-form video feed of Simple Wikipedia articles. Swipe vertically, listen with your device’s text-to-speech, and read colorful word-by-word captions.
